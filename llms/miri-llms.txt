# Miri

> Miri (Miri AI) is an AI-powered health and wellness engagement platform based in Oakland, California. It sits between clinical touchpoints as a daily AI companion — logging meals via text or photo, tracking goals and body measurements, and running coach check-ins — while a behavioral-intelligence layer surfaces at-risk users and revenue opportunities for digital health, telehealth, GLP-1/medical weight-loss, lab-diagnostics, and supplement companies. Developers embed Miri through a React Native Component SDK and a server-side, API-key-authenticated API with configurable webhooks. The developer program is currently in alpha.
